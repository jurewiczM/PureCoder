D · Current mock

Maroon on black, pure #c41818 accent. Everything is tinted red, so red cannot also mean failure — the badge and the brand fight for the same colour.

My pick: A. The mock's real problem is not the hue, it's that the accent is spent on decoration — headers, borders, brand, and the failure badge are all the same red, so nothing is left to signal a refused run. A neutral base with one warm accent frees green/amber/red to mean only pass, retry and refusal, which is the only thing this UI has to communicate. B is the interesting outsider if you want it to look like nothing else.
